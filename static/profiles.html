Auto-Optimize Line Spacing
Adjusts lines per page (23–28) to minimize blank lines on the final page of the transcript body. The standard is 25 lines per page, but if your transcript leaves the last page mostly empty, Notare tries nearby values to fill pages more evenly. Line height adjusts automatically to stay within court-acceptable spacing. Only affects the Standard Deposition export.
