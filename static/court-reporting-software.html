The workflow

Notare is built around how the work actually moves — five workspaces you can use on their own or run as one connected pipeline.

Live capture & real-time

Record the proceeding and follow a live draft as it happens — useful for readbacks and for handing attorneys a rough in real time.

AI transcription

Turn the audio into a first draft automatically. Use the bundled on-device engine, or bring your own cloud key for higher-accuracy speech recognition.

Delivery

FAQ

Common questions

Is Notare digital court reporting software?

Yes. Notare is built for digital court reporters and transcribers — capture, real-time draft, transcription, editing, proofreading and delivery in one Windows application.

Does it do real-time reporting?

Yes. The reporting workspace gives you a live draft as the proceeding is recorded, which you can use for readbacks and rough deliveries.

Can I keep audio on my own machine?

Yes — choose the bundled on-device engine and the audio stays local. If you prefer higher-accuracy cloud recognition, you can bring your own key instead. Your transcripts and case files are always stored locally either way.

How is it priced?

Flat per-user pricing by workspace or bundle — no transcript limits, no overage fees, and no per-minute charges from Notare. Pay for the parts of the workflow you actually use.
