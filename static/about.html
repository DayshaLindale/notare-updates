About Notare

Notare exists because the legal transcription industry is changing. and the tools available haven't kept pace with what working professionals actually need.

Automatic speech recognition is a productivity aid. It's a powerful one. But every ASR tool on the market produces output that requires substantial human review and correction before it's anywhere near filing-ready. Speaker misidentification, formatting gaps, mishears, and context errors are known, recurring, and expected. That hasn't changed with newer models. it's improved, but it hasn't been solved.

What has been missing is a tool that understands the workflow that happens after the audio is transcribed. The formatting. The jurisdiction-specific rules. The template compliance. The speaker attribution. The proofing. That's where reporters spend their time. and that's what Notare was built to address.

Our Approach to AI

We are not building AI to replace transcriptionists. We are implementing AI in the most efficient and practical ways possible. as a productivity aid that respects the professional's role in the process.

ASR output is a starting point, not a finished product. The contractor's responsibility to produce a complete and accurate transcript does not change because a tool generated the first draft. Notare makes that first draft better. better formatted, better attributed, better organized. so the human review process is faster and more focused.

The integrity of the legal record depends on human oversight. Every transcript Notare produces is a draft that requires professional verification before certification or filing. We are transparent about this because accuracy in the legal field is not aspirational. it is mandatory.
