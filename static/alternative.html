FAQ

Common questions

Why would I switch?

If you're paying per minute, paying for modules you don't use, or uncomfortable with your record living on a vendor's cloud, Notare offers a flat per-user, pay-for-what-you-use, local-first alternative.

Will I be locked into a subscription?

Notare uses clear flat pricing with monthly or annual options, and the app runs on your machine. There's no per-minute meter and no lock-in on your files.

Do I have to buy the whole suite?

No. The five workspaces are available individually or as bundles, so you only pay for the parts of the workflow you actually use.

Does my audio go to the cloud?

Only if you choose a cloud recognition engine. Notare also ships with an on-device engine that keeps audio on your machine, and your transcripts and case files are stored locally either way.
